Colophon

Hindu Festivals Atlas, three folios: I Deepavali; II Navaratri · Durga Puja · Dussehra; III Pongal · Makar Sankranti. Every factual statement carries a numbered marker that opens its source; unnumbered sentences that draw connections are the atlas's own synthesis and are marked synthesis where they matter. Indian institutions and authors were consulted first. Sources were accessed 22–23 October 2026.

Plates are drawn in code as graphite studies and are placeholders for commissioned or generated illustrations; none of them is historical evidence.

How it was made

Created by Mohan Boggara and built with AI assistance (Claude and ChatGPT) under his direction: research gathered from the cited sources, text written for the atlas, and plates drawn in code, with generation prompts kept for later illustrations.
