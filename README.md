# Hindu Festivals Atlas

A pencil-drawn atlas of Hindu festivals in three folios, with every factual claim cited to its source.

**Live site:** https://mboggara-personal.github.io/hindu-festivals-atlas/

## What's inside

- **Folio I · Deepavali:** the stories behind the festival, the five days, how the date is set, the threshold ritual, an India map of regional practice, a timeline, a symbol cabinet, food, a relationship map, comparisons and Deepavali abroad.
- **Folio II · Navaratri · Durga Puja · Dussehra:** six stories, the days from Mahalaya to Kojagara, one lunar month drawn as a ring from Mahalaya to Diwali, the making of a Kolkata Durga image, golu, and fourteen regions.
- **Folio III · Pongal · Makar Sankranti:** the four days of Pongal, Lohri, Magh Bihu, Uttarayan and more; a "Sun's clock" showing how the Sun's entry into Makara has drifted from the winter solstice over the centuries.
- **Sources & Evidence:** 787 cited claims from 100 sources, with Indian institutions and authors consulted first. Each claim carries a confidence level and a knowledge type, and open questions are listed rather than guessed.

Dark theme by default, with a light toggle. Your theme choice is remembered in your own browser; nothing else is stored.

## Files

`docs/index.html` is the whole atlas: one self-contained page with its data baked in, served by GitHub Pages from the `docs/` folder. `docs/og.jpg` is the share-preview image.
