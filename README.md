# Hindu Festivals Atlas

A pencil-drawn atlas of Hindu festivals in seven folios, with every factual claim cited to its source.

**Live site:** https://hindu-festivals-atlas.ekaneka.org/

## What's inside

- **Folio I · Deepavali:** the stories behind the festival, the five days, how the date is set, the threshold ritual, an India map of regional practice, a timeline, a symbol cabinet, food, a relationship map, comparisons and Deepavali abroad.
- **Folio II · Navaratri · Durga Puja · Dussehra:** six stories, the days from Mahalaya to Kojagara, one lunar month drawn as a ring from Mahalaya to Diwali, the making of a Kolkata Durga image, golu, and fourteen regions.
- **Folio III · Pongal · Makar Sankranti:** the four days of Pongal, Lohri, Magh Bihu, Uttarayan and more; a "Sun's clock" showing how the Sun's entry into Makara has drifted from the winter solstice over the centuries.
- **Folio IV · Holi:** six stories, from Braj's week to Rang Pañcamī, and a ring of lunar days showing how this full moon closes the year in one reckoning.
- **Folio V · Ganesh Chaturthi:** Gowri Habba to Anant Chaturdashi, the public festival since the 1890s, and the courts on what idols may be immersed.
- **Folio VI · Onam:** ten stars from Atham to Thiruvōṇam on a star wheel, the Onasadya, and Onam beside Bali Padyami.
- **Folio VII · the spring New Years:** Ugadi, Gudi Padwa, Navreh, Cheti Chand, Puthandu, Vishu, Bihu, Pana Sankranti and Pohela Boishakh, and the rules that turn one Sankranti into different dates.
- **Sources & Evidence:** 1,344 cited claims from 201 sources, with Indian institutions and authors consulted first. Each claim carries a confidence level and a knowledge type, and open questions are listed rather than guessed.

Dark theme by default, with a light toggle. Your theme choice is remembered in your own browser; nothing else is stored. Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter: no cookies, no personal data.
