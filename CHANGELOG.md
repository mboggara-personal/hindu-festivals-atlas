# Changelog

## v1.1 — 15 November 2026

Four new folios, making seven:

- **Folio IV · Holi:** a ring of lunar days from Phālguna to Chaitra Śukla 1, switchable between full-moon and new-moon month endings; Braj, Dol, Dola, Hola Mohalla, Shigmo, Yaoshang, Kaman Pandigai and Phagwa abroad.
- **Folio V · Ganesh Chaturthi:** the bright fortnight of Bhādrapada against the 2026 dates for each household duration; Gowri Habba, Goa's matoli, Khairatabad, Lalbaug; a "Water" leaf on the plaster-of-Paris idol litigation.
- **Folio VI · Onam:** a wheel of the 27 lunar stars inside Kerala's solar months, Atham to Thiruvōṇam; places, the Onasadya, and a comparison with Bali Padyami.
- **Folio VII · the spring New Years:** Ugadi's moving date against Mesha Sankranti, and a slider showing how the Odia, Tamil, Kerala and Bengal rules turn one Sankranti into different dates.

Now 1,344 cited claims from 201 sources. The footer reads "© 2026 Mohan Boggara (Personal)".
